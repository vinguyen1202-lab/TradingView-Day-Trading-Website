Read the candles like a beginner

1
Direction: daily 9 EMA above/below 50 SMA; compare stock performance against SPY.
2
Location: premarket and previous-day levels are our potential floor/ceiling.
3
Confirmation: the five-minute candle must finish past the level (or retest it) with volume.
4
Risk: the structural stop marks where the stock setup may fail. The calculator estimates option loss from your stop assumption and fees; no preset dollar cap is applied.

Choose a pattern

Five easy rules

  1. Check the daily trend and SPY before watching the entry.
  2. Draw premarket high/low and previous-day high/low.
  3. Only trust a candle after its five-minute period ends.
  4. Require meaningful volume and a clear stop/target.
  5. Review an actual option's bid/ask and calculated risk before any paper trade.
A candle pattern is not a guarantee. The Pine indicator uses New York exchange time for its rules; its 9:35–11:00 a.m. entry window is 6:35–8:00 a.m. Pacific Time. Website timestamps adjust between PST and PDT. The indicator stays at WAIT unless its filters all pass.

Set up the TradingView signal bridge

1
Open TradingView Supercharts and select your stock's 5-minute chart. Enable extended-hours bars.
2
Open Pine Editor and paste the included MarketPilot_TV_Signals.pine script. Save and add it to your chart. The values and REVIEW labels will appear inside TradingView, even if you don't have webhooks.
3
Publish this Python app to Render using the included render.yaml file. Render can generate TV_WEBHOOK_TOKEN automatically. View the secret in your Render settings and keep it private. GitHub + Render permission is required to publish, and a free Render instance may sleep and miss webhooks.
4
If your TradingView plan includes webhook alerts, create a new alert with condition MarketPilot → Any alert() function call, and set Webhook URL to https://YOUR-DOMAIN/api/tradingview/YOUR_PRIVATE_TOKEN. Enable 2FA as required by TradingView.
5
Repeat the alert for other symbols you want monitored. Each one needs a stock chart and alert.
Your paid TradingView plan may include alert webhooks. Enable TradingView two-factor authentication, then create a separate alert for each stock. The connected ChatGPT MCP authorization does not automatically authorize a public website.
